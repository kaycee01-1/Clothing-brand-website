Where it began

Our Story

We started with a simple idea: everyday clothing shouldn’t have to sacrifice comfort for style.

What began as a passion for creating pieces we would actually want to wear grew into a brand built around quality, confidence and individuality.

Every design starts with the same question: would we reach for this on a regular Tuesday? Does it fit the way we want it to? Will it still feel right after the hundredth wear? If the answer is no, it doesn’t make it out of the studio.

We obsess over the details most people only notice when they feel them: the weight of a fabric, the drape of a sleeve, the way a seam sits against your skin.

We’re still the same people who care a little too much about a good hoodie. The difference is that now you’re part of it.
